## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, calm, and distraction-free cognitive environment tailored for students, academics, and self-directed learners. Preparing for critical exams demands intense mental clarity; the interface counters cognitive overload and exam fatigue by merging Scandinavian minimalism with structural, modern productivity architecture. 

The aesthetic is anchored by organic, botanical teals, deep slate depths, and balanced sage accents. This evokes a sense of sustained focus, quiet confidence, and steady academic momentum rather than the anxiety typically associated with deadline-driven study. Surfaces are crisp, uncluttered, and purposeful, prioritizing effortless reading ergonomics, structured knowledge synthesis, and transparent workflow tracking across Android and desktop form factors.

## Colors

The color palette directly translates the tonal hierarchy established by deep teal, abyssal slate, and botanical green tones into functional, high-contrast user interfaces:

- **Primary (`#12544F`):** Deep Teal acts as the primary anchor for core actions, persistent navigation sidebars, prominent button elements, and primary focal points.
- **Secondary (`#2A835F`):** Emerald Accent drives progress indicators, positive review state indicators, active tabs, and secondary interactive calls to action.
- **Tertiary (`#8BBB92`):** Soft Mint provides gentle contextual highlights, subtle tags, completed module backdrops, and active selection pills without competing for visual hierarchy.
- **Neutral (`#092328`):** Abyssal Slate governs high-contrast body typography, structural headings, deep framing lines, and dark-mode backdrop baselines.
- **Named Surfaces & Accents:** Light application states rely on `#F9FBFB` for foundation canvas backing, `#FFFFFF` for elevated modular cards, and `#E1EAE7` for feather-light dividers. Informative states utilize dedicated warning amber and crisp crimson for quiz results and validation errors.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all textual hierarchies. Its geometric proportions, balanced aperture, and friendly clarity deliver optimal readability during long study sessions and rapid scanability across quiz review flows.

Headline styles deploy tighter letter-spacing and medium-to-bold weights to anchor section entry points and dashboards cleanly. Long-form summary and reading notes enforce generous line-height multiples (`1.6x` to `1.65x`) to preserve optical tracking across desktop columns and responsive mobile viewports. Small badges, status indicators, and metadata tags utilize capitalized or tightened label scales with widened letter spacing to ensure crisp rendering at compact sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an 8-point base spatial rhythm designed to maintain strict alignment between responsive Android touch layouts and widescreen PC desktop dashboards:

- **Desktop Form Factor:** Employs a structured 12-column layout with a 260px fixed primary navigation sidebar, dynamic multi-pane work zones (e.g., dual-pane split for reading source files while generating smart summaries), and 24px grid gutters bounded by 32px margins.
- **Mobile Form Factor (Android):** Collapses into a single-column stacked view with a persistent bottom navigation bar, 16px lateral canvas gutters, and edge-to-edge reading cards.
- **Spatial Rhythm:**
  - `space-xs` (4px): Micro gaps between icons and labels, subtle pill badges.
  - `space-sm` (8px): Gaps in question option blocks, button icon-to-text spacing, compact list padding.
  - `space-md` (16px): Standard internal card padding, input group stacking.
  - `space-lg` (24px): Inter-section spacing, quiz header-to-question separation.
  - `space-xl` (40px): Hero group divisions and empty-state vertical cushioning.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal surface stacking** paired with **soft, tinted ambient contact shadows**. The system avoids harsh skeuomorphic shadows or aggressive black drop-shadows, ensuring surfaces feel feather-light and focused:

- **Surface Base (Level 0):** Canvas backdrop (`#F9FBFB`), flat with no shadow.
- **Raised Cards (Level 1):** Workspace cards, note list items, and summary containers sit at Level 1, utilizing a subtle border (`1px solid #E1EAE7`) combined with an ambient teal-tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(9, 35, 40, 0.05)`.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts (Level 2):** Study schedule drawers, material upload dialogs, and question jump sheets utilize: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(9, 35, 40, 0.12)` paired with a soft background overlay (`rgba(9, 35, 40, 0.4)`).
- **Interactive State (Hover/Focus):** Clickable elements translate upward by `1px` with an enriched shadow: `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(18, 84, 79, 0.10)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Balanced Rounded** visual geometry (Level 2). This delivers structured efficiency suitable for disciplined study routines while keeping corner radii inviting and ergonomic:

- **Standard UI Elements (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Form fields, quiz option choices, action buttons, and individual list tiles.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Examination score cards, summary generation previews, file upload drop zones, and schedule calendar blocks.
- **Overlays & Large Surfaces (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Desktop feature panels, Android bottom sheets, and modal containers.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Filter tags, subject taxonomy chips, completion percentage pills, and timer counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#12544F` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Height is 44px (touch-friendly on mobile), with an 8px radius. Active state transitions to `#092328`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent surface with a 1.5px border of `#12544F` and `#12544F` text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border or background; text in `#12544F`. Hover introduces `#EBF4F0`.
- **Icon Buttons:** 40x40px interactive boundary with centered 20px icons, padded with 8px corner radius.

### Input Fields & Upload Drops
- **Inputs:** Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid border (`#E1EAE7`), 8px border-radius, 12px horizontal padding, 14px text. Focus state activates a 1.5px border in `#2A835F` with a soft 3px focus ring tint (`rgba(42, 131, 95, 0.15)`).
- **File Upload Area:** Dashed 2px border using `#8BBB92`, background tint of `#F9FBFB`, hosting icon indicators, drag-and-drop feedback states, and upload progress rails.

### Cards & Modules
- **Material & Note Cards:** White canvas, 16px border-radius, `1px solid #E1EAE7`, padded by 20px. Includes header metadata (subject chip, date), body preview, and quick-action menu pinned top-right.
- **Quiz Interactive Card:** Clean white card encapsulating the question stem, question progress ticker (`Q 3 of 20`), and stacked option cards.
- **Option Selectors:** Neutral option container with `1px solid #E1EAE7`. Selected state adopts `#EBF4F0` background and a `#2A835F` border. Correct response states reveal `#1B7E5A` accents with explanatory sub-text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Subject Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with 4px vertical and 12px horizontal. Utilizes `#EBF4F0` background with `#12544F` label text.
- **Status Badges:** Small pill tags for study session indicators: "Selesai" (Finished: `#8BBB92` tint), "Sedang Berjalan" (In Progress: emerald border), "Terlewat" (Missed: light crimson tint).

### Progress & Timers
- **Progress Bars:** Continuous linear bars with a 6px height and rounded caps. The track uses `#E1EAE7` with active fill driven by `#2A835F`.
- **Countdown Display:** High-contrast mono-spaced digital numeric displays framed in `#092328` surfaces with `#8BBB92` glowing accents for active exam practice sessions.
